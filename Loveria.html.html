Step-by-step guide

This is a local demo prototype. Real accounts, messages, and profile pictures will need a backend/database when we build the full production app.
  1. Create an account using an email address or phone number.
  2. Open your profile by clicking your name in the top-right.
  3. Add a profile picture, edit your name, and write a bio.
  4. Get a blue tick in your profile for demo verification.
  5. Create a novel in Writer Studio (give it a title and description).
  6. Click "✍️ Write now" to write chapters for your novel.
  7. Like novels and add favorites in the Reader Portal.
  8. Read novels chapter by chapter by opening a novel and clicking a chapter.
  9. Follow people by opening their profile and pressing Follow.
  10. Message people by opening their profile and pressing Message.
  11. Share profiles using the Share button on any profile.
  12. Block or unblock users from their profile if needed.
  13. Toggle music with the Music button for a calm reading atmosphere.
